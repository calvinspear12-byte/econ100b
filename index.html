How this works

Weights (syllabus): midterm 35%, final 40%, 5 section quizzes 25%. Midterm is 80 points: 8 multiple choice at 5 each plus 2 free response at 20 each.

Curve: Frech says there is no fixed scale and he curves toward other 100B sections. So your letter depends on where you rank. This page estimates the class's course grades, then applies a real past letter split from the UCSB Registrar.

Harsh curve: Spring 2024 ECON 100B split (17.8% A range), and it assumes the class averages 55% on the final and 95% on quizzes.

Honest curve: all regular ECON 100B sections 2022 to 2026 combined (3,689 grades, 25.4% A range), class averages 45% on the final (same as the midterm) and 88% on quizzes.

Known: midterm class median 45%, mean 45.5%. Everything about the final is an estimate, so treat letters as a guide, not a promise.

Data: UCSB Registrar grade counts (Daily Nexus, public records)
